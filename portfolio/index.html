About

I'm Daniel Marshall, a Sydney photographer specialising in macro and nature work. I photograph small things up close, then stack dozens of frames into one image so every hair, scale and edge is in focus.

  • OM System OM-1 Mark II
  • 90mm macro lens
  • Zerene Stacker focus stacking
  • Godox flash and scene lighting
